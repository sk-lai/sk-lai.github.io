LAI Sing Kwong

Undergraduate student @ HKUST · Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence

Hi, welcome! I am LAI Sing Kwong (賴星光), an undergraduate student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), majoring in Computer Science with an extended major in Artificial Intelligence.

I am currently working on AI for Quant in Prof. Yi R. (May) Fung’s group, mentored by Zhaochen Su. Previously, I collaborated with Zeyu LIU on AI for finance in Prof. Song Guo’s group.

My research interests include AI for Quant, LLM agents, and reinforcement learning. I am also keen to explore a broader range of research directions in AI. If you are interested in collaboration, feel free to reach out!
